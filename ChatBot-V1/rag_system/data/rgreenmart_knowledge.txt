HEALTHY TIPS
================================================================================

ALMONDS (Badam):
Rich in Vitamin E, magnesium, and healthy fats. Supports heart health by
reducing LDL cholesterol. Promotes gut health supporting beneficial bacteria
including Bifidobacteria and Lactobacillus. Reduces inflammatory markers and
improves blood sugar control. Great calcium source (75mg per 30g serving).

CASHEWS:
High in antioxidants protecting against free radical damage. Lower in fat than
many nuts. Helps increase antioxidant capacity in the body. Good source of
heart-healthy fats and essential minerals like zinc and magnesium.

RAISINS (Kismish):
Natural concentrated energy source. Rich in dietary fiber and natural sugars.
Contains iron, potassium, and antioxidants. Supports digestive health and
provides a quick natural energy boost.

DATES:
Naturally sweet and energy-dense, excellent natural sweetener alternative.
High in dietary fiber and potassium. Supports digestive health. Rich in
antioxidants and essential minerals. Provides sustained energy release.

BLACK DATES:
Premium variety of dates with more polyphenols than regular dates. Particularly
rich in antioxidants. Excellent natural energy source and supports overall health.

HONEY:
Natural sweetener with strong antimicrobial properties. Rich in antioxidants.
Supports immune health. Has wound-healing properties. Used in traditional medicine
for centuries. RGreenMart offers pure, unadulterated premium-grade honey.

PISTACHIOS (Pista):
Highest protein content among nuts: 6 grams per 1-ounce (28g) serving.
Close to 90 percent of fats are healthy mono- and polyunsaturated type.
Improves blood lipid levels and lowers the LDL to HDL cholesterol ratio.
Helps maintain normal blood sugar levels after meals. High in potassium.

WALNUTS:
Excellent source of omega-3 fatty acids. Rich in copper and manganese.
Eating 1 to 2 ounces (28-57g) per day may improve brain function and reduce
risk factors for dementia. Reduces LDL cholesterol and blood pressure.
Improves artery health and reduces inflammation throughout the body.

FIGS (Atthipalam):
Rich in dietary fiber, calcium, iron, potassium, and magnesium. Supports bone
health and digestive health. Has natural laxative properties. Naturally sweet,
serves as a healthy dessert alternative. Good source of plant-based minerals.

APRICOTS:
High in beta-carotene which converts to Vitamin A supporting eye and immune
health. Good source of potassium and dietary fiber. Supports skin health and
helps maintain healthy blood pressure. Rich in antioxidants. Supports bone health.

GENERAL HEALTHY SNACKING TIPS:
- A small handful (28g or 1 oz) is the standard serving for nuts and dried fruits
- Adults should aim for 4 to 6 servings of unsalted nuts per week
- Research shows nuts help reduce inflammation related to heart disease and diabetes
- Regularly eating nuts can improve artery health and lower blood pressure
- Store nuts in the freezer to extend shelf life and prevent fats from going rancid
- Choose unsalted and unsweetened nuts for maximum heart health benefit
- Replace unhealthy snacks with nuts to support weight management
- Nuts are excellent plant-based sources of protein, fiber, healthy fats, vitamins,
  and minerals including zinc, magnesium, calcium, potassium, and selenium

SERVING SIZES AND DAILY RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

HOW MANY NUTS TO EAT PER DAY:
The recommended daily serving size for nuts is 28 grams or 1 oz which is
approximately one small handful. Adults should aim for 4 to 6 servings of
unsalted nuts per week for maximum health benefit. A daily handful of mixed
nuts is considered ideal for heart health and overall nutrition.

DAILY SERVING GUIDE:
- Almonds (Badam)    : 6 to 8 almonds per day, 28g serving
- Cashews (Kaju)     : 4 to 5 cashews per day, 28g serving
- Walnuts (Akhrot)   : 1 to 2 walnuts per day, 28g to 57g serving
- Pistachios (Pista) : 1 oz or 28g per day, about 49 kernels
- Raisins (Kismish)  : 1 small handful or 28g per day
- Dates (Khajoor)    : 2 to 3 dates per day for energy
- Honey              : 1 to 2 teaspoons per day as natural sweetener
- Figs (Atthipalam)  : 2 to 3 dried figs per day
- Apricot            : 3 to 5 dried apricots per day
- Black Dates        : 2 to 3 black dates per day

GENERAL RULE: A small handful (28g or 1 oz) is the standard serving for
all nuts and dried fruits. This equals roughly 1/4 cup of nuts.
